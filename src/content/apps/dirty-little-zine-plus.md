Lay out an 8-page fold-and-cut zine from your own photos and export a
print-ready PDF. Photos are decoded, filtered and written into the PDF inside
the tab. There is no backend and nothing is uploaded.

Inspired by [dirtylittlezine.com](https://dirtylittlezine.com), rewritten to
add multi-photo pages, print filters, zoom and a PDF that is not flattened.

## What it adds

- One to six photos per page across ten layout templates.
- Pan on both axes plus 1x to 6x zoom, anchored at the cursor.
- Eleven filter presets, five print effects and eight adjustment sliders.
- Per-photo JPEGs with transparent text overlays, rather than one flattened
  sheet image.
- Letter, A4, A3 and Tabloid paper.
- A QR code on any page, in one of nine positions, sized in millimeters.

## Printing it

Print single-sided, cut the center slit, and fold. The page order in the PDF
is already arranged for the fold, so no rearranging is needed.
